Frequently asked questions

How far ahead should I book?

We recommend four to six weeks, and earlier for December to March.

Can I change a package?

Yes. Tell us what you want to add or remove and we will update the quote.

How do I pay?

We take a small deposit to confirm your booking. The rest is paid on arrival.

Are the tours safe for families?

Yes. We use licensed drivers and can arrange child seats on request.
