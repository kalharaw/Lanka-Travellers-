About Lanka Travellers

Lanka Travellers is a Colombo-based travel agency that makes exploring Sri Lanka simple, safe and memorable. We began with one guide and one van, and we still plan every tour by hand.

Our Vision

To be the most trusted way to discover Sri Lanka, known for honest advice and warm hospitality.

Our Mission

To create well-planned, responsible tours that give travellers real experiences and support local communities.

Why Choose Lanka Travellers?

  • Local experts. Our guides grew up in the places you visit.
  • Clear prices. No hidden fees. What we quote is what you pay.
  • Flexible plans. We adjust every tour to your pace and budget.
  • 24/7 support. A real person answers when you need help on the road.

Our Services

  • Tour packages and custom itineraries
  • Hotel and homestay booking
  • Private cars with English-speaking drivers
  • Airport pick-up and drop-off
  • Train and domestic transport tickets
  • Cultural, wildlife and adventure activities

Our promise to Sri Lanka

We hire local guides and drivers, use family-run guesthouses where we can, and ask every guest to leave places as they found them.
